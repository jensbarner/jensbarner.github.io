About

Jens Oliver Barner

Author of Was wirklich zählt. Music and spoken projects carry the same thought forward: orientation after crises, health, mental strength and mindful life balance.

I am Jens Oliver Barner. Writing is a way for me to bring order into experiences - and to shape something from them that can offer orientation to others - especially people who want to realign after pressure, exhaustion or change. I am interested in which decisions carry us, which habits strengthen us and what truly matters in the end.

Professionally, I work as a leader. Alongside that, I focus on clarity, mindful living and personal responsibility. My texts connect analytical thinking with practical experience from health, self-organization and inner stability.

In 2017, I began writing down thoughts for my children. Over the years, private notes became a personal compass - and eventually the foundation for my first book: Was wirklich zählt. It moves from outer appearance toward inner truth and asks how clarity, health, mental strength and life balance emerge in our own thinking, actions and lives.

Health, responsibility, freedom, focus and mindful decisions are not theoretical topics for me. They show up in everyday life: in sleep, energy, relationships, work, setbacks and new beginnings.

Since 2026, the same thoughts have also taken a musical form. What Really Matters translates the guiding idea of the book into a calm song about orientation, reduction and closeness to what is essential.

The texts, themes and artistic direction come from me. Modern AI tools help me develop musical forms from them - as an extension of my thoughts, not as a replacement for attitude or content.

Through books, audiobook projects and music, I want to encourage people to pause, take burnout signals seriously, realign their inner compass and decide more consciously what should truly have space in their lives.
