About

Jens Oliver Barner

Author of Was wirklich zählt. Books, music and spoken projects carry the same thought forward: orientation after crises, health, mental strength and mindful life balance.

Writing is a way for me to bring order into experiences and shape something that can give orientation to others, especially people who want to realign after stress, exhaustion or change.

Professionally, I work as a leader. Alongside that, I focus on clarity, mindful living and personal responsibility. My texts connect analytical thinking with practical experience from health, self-organization and inner stability.

In 2017 I began writing down thoughts for my children. Over the years, private notes became a personal compass and eventually the foundation for my first book: Was wirklich zählt.
