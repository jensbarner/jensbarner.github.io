Music as an extension of thought

The songs translate central motifs from the book and texts into a musical form. They are about moments when outside expectations become quieter, vulnerability becomes visible and inner strength finds a calmer language.

What Really Matters is close to the guiding idea of Was wirklich zählt. Soap Bubble Rise extends the imagery toward fragile beginnings, quiet movement and the strength that can emerge from continuing onward.
