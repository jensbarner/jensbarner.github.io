The Voice Inside

Jens Oliver Barner

An emotional cinematic folk-pop song about the quiet voice that remains when the world becomes too loud.

"The Voice Inside" follows the moment when outside expectations, speed and old roles begin to drown out your own inner signal. The song turns back toward stillness, clarity and trust.

Soft acoustic guitar, warm piano, airy pads and gentle strings carry a simple, memorable hook. The verses stay intimate while the refrain opens into a bright and hopeful lift.

At the center is the inner compass: not becoming louder, but listening more clearly.
