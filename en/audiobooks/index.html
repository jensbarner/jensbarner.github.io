Why a spoken edition?

Some thoughts unfold differently when you hear them. The planned audiobook will translate Was wirklich zählt into a calm, focused form - suitable for walks, travel or mindful breaks in everyday life.

The spoken edition is intended not only to read the book aloud, but to preserve its quiet character: orientation, clarity and inner strength as an audible companion.
