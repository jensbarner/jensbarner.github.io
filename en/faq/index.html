FAQ

Questions & Answers

Short answers to frequently asked questions about the book, target readers, music, audiobook projects, working method and contact options.

What is Was wirklich zählt about?

Orientation after crises, clarity, health, mental strength and mindful life balance. The book asks what carries us when outside expectations become quieter and our own compass becomes more important again.

Who is the book suitable for?

For people who want to realign after a setback, prevent burnout, live more consciously or, as people with responsibility in family, work and everyday life, seek more balance instead of merely functioning.

Is it also intended for people with responsibility in everyday life?

Yes. For people who carry a lot: for children, family, work, partnership, care, a team or their own environment. The book asks how responsibility can be taken without losing sight of oneself.

Is Was wirklich zählt a classic self-help book?

No. It is not about quick recipes or loud success formulas. The book is more of a quiet compass: personal, reflective and close to questions that do not always have simple answers in real life.

How did Was wirklich zählt come into being?

The first thoughts began in 2017 after a bicycle accident. On October 10, 2025, the decision was made to turn them into a book. Around five months of intensive, almost daily work on language, structure and content followed.

Why was the book published now?

Of course, it would have been possible to keep optimizing for much longer. But every project eventually needs a clear ending. The beginning of spring felt especially fitting as a symbol of new beginnings, growth and clarity.

How extensive is the book?

Was wirklich zählt has 246 pages, 7,299 lines and 65,714 words. For number lovers: 363,335 characters without spaces.

Can the book help with exhaustion or burnout signals?

It can help you pause, become more aware of stress and take warning signs seriously. It does not replace medical or therapeutic help, but it can be a companion impulse to listen to yourself again.

How are the book, music and audiobook connected?

All projects revolve around the same core: orientation, reduction and the question of what should truly have space in life.

What role does AI play in the music?

The texts, themes and artistic direction come from me. AI tools help develop musical forms from these thoughts.

Where can I buy the book?

The book can be ordered from all common bookshops and also online, including Amazon, Thalia and Hugendubel. Current Amazon purchase link: Was wirklich zählt at Amazon. The book page also leads to the reading sample.

Which publisher released the book?

Was wirklich zählt was published through BoD - Books on Demand. The book can be ordered from all common bookshops and online. The ISBN-13 is 978-3695700738, the ISBN-10 is 3695700734.

How does BoD - Books on Demand work?

BoD - Books on Demand has specialized in book printing and print on demand since 1998. This means: a book is usually printed only after an order has been placed.

As a result, no large print run has to be produced and stored in advance. More information is available at bod.de.

How long are delivery times?

Because Was wirklich zählt is produced by print on demand, delivery times can be a little longer than for books already in stock. Sometimes, however, the book arrives faster than stated by the shop.

The exact delivery time depends on the respective bookshop or online store. In a very fast time, this can feel unfamiliar, but it is part of the principle of printing on demand. Perhaps this small waiting time can also be a moment of slowing down: in an extremely fast time, anticipation is sometimes the most beautiful joy.

Where can I listen to the music?
