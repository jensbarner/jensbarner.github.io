FAQ

Questions & Answers

Short answers about the book, target readers, availability, music and contact.

What is Was wirklich zählt about?

About orientation after crises, clarity, health, mental strength and mindful life balance.

Who is the book for?

For people who want to realign after a setback, prevent burnout, live more consciously or find balance while carrying responsibility.

Is it a classic self-help book?

No. It is less about quick formulas and more a quiet compass based on personal experience and reflection.

Where can I buy the book?

The book can be ordered through common bookshops and online shops. Current Amazon link: Was wirklich zählt at Amazon.

Publisher and ISBN

The book was published through BoD - Books on Demand. ISBN-13: 978-3695700738. ISBN-10: 3695700734.

How does BoD work?

BoD works with print on demand. The book is usually printed after an order, so no large stock has to be produced in advance.

How long are delivery times?

Delivery can take a little longer than books already in stock, but sometimes the book arrives faster than stated by the shop.

Where can I listen to the music?

The music is available on major platforms and stores, including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, TIDAL, TikTok, Instagram, iHeartRadio, Pandora and more. Distribution is handled through DistroKid.
